02 // PROFILE

Engineering with a system view.

I am an Electrical Engineering graduate with a concentration in Control Electronics. My project experience spans embedded firmware, sensor integration, wireless telemetry, industrial communication, automation, and real-time monitoring.

I enjoy working across the boundary between hardware and software: reading physical signals, designing firmware logic, connecting devices through industrial protocols, and turning measurements into useful information through APIs and dashboards.

My portfolio reflects hands-on engineering work rather than a single software stack. The common thread is practical system integration, debugging, measurement, and making embedded systems behave predictably.
